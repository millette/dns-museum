David Leadbeater’s historical service exposed Wikipedia lookups through DNS. A name encoded the search target, and the result came back in a DNS answer. The service ended in early 2024.

The project made a familiar knowledge source available to a very small client. It remains an early example of DNS as a text retrieval interface.
