Randall Munroe’s xkcd comic “Google Announcement” imagines Google shutting down its other products so it can focus on the 8.8.8.8 public DNS server.

It is not a separate DNS protocol or service. It belongs in the talk as a small cultural artefact about the visibility of public resolvers.
